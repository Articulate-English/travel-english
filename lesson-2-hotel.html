Hotel & Accommodation

Travel vocabulary for Italian speakers

Key vocabulary (click for Italian definition):

reservation · check-in · check-out · room service · concierge · single room · double room · suite

Questions:

What facilities do you expect in a good hotel room?

Wi-Fi, TV, minibar, safe, toiletries, towels, phone, AC. Luxury hotels add bathrobes, slippers, and premium amenities. The room should be clean and comfortable.

Order the words:

[I / have / booked / a / double / room / for / three / nights]

I have booked a double room for three nights at the Grand Hotel downtown.

Describe the picture:

The room has two beds with crisp white linens. A large window provides natural light. The modern decor includes a flat-screen TV and desk area for work.

Speaking question:

Describe your worst hotel experience. What went wrong?

The air conditioning was broken, the room was noisy, and housekeeping forgot to restock toiletries. The reception offered a partial refund and moved us to a quieter floor.

Discussion question:

What makes a hotel truly memorable for guests?

Exceptional service, clean rooms, great location, comfortable beds, reliable Wi-Fi, and personal touches like welcome notes or room upgrades for loyalty members.

Speaking · 2 minutes:

Describe your ideal hotel. What features make it special?

Panoramic city views, king-size bed, spa bathroom, 24-hour room service, rooftop pool, and concierge who can secure restaurant reservations and theater tickets.

Vocabulary review - B2+ words:

occupancy · amenities · reception · housekeeping

Grammar focus:

Your room is cleaned daily.
Breakfast is served from 7 to 10.
The bill was sent to your email.

Your reservation has been confirmed. The concierge will recommend restaurants nearby. Housekeeping updates the room weekly.

Useful expressions:

"Do you have availability?" · "Is breakfast included?" · "Can I have late checkout?" · "Where is the gym?"

Cultural tip:

In Italy, family-run hotels offer personal hospitality. Staff may not speak fluent English. Learn basic phrases like 'La prenotazione' and 'Il conto per favore' to ease communication.
