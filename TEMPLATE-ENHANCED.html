Questions · Answer in full sentences:

What documents do you need to check in at the airport?

You need your passport or ID, boarding pass, and sometimes a visa depending on the destination. The check-in counter staff will verify your identity and issue baggage tags.

Deeper questions:

How can airports improve the check-in experience for international travelers?

Multilingual staff, clearer signage, mobile check-in apps, and better baggage handling systems would help. Priority lanes for elderly or disabled passengers could reduce bottlenecks. Real-time delay notifications via SMS would also improve the experience.

Speaking · 2 minutes:

Describe the most efficient airport you've traveled through. What made it stand out? Would you recommend it for business travelers?

Zurich airport impressed me with its punctuality and clear signage in multiple languages. The underground train to the city center was convenient. For business travelers, the lounges and fast Wi-Fi are essential. I'd definitely recommend it for connections.

Useful expressions:

Cultural tip:

In Italy, airport staff appreciate politeness. Greet them with "Buongiorno" before asking for help. Italians often gesture while speaking, so body language is important for communication.
