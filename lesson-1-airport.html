Airport & Check-in

Travel vocabulary for Italian speakers · B1 + B2 level

Key vocabulary (click for Italian definition):

boarding pass · baggage allowance · departure gate · carry-on · flight delay · check-in counter · arrival hall · connecting flight

Questions · Answer in full sentences:

What documents do you need to check in at the airport?

You need your passport or ID, boarding pass, and sometimes a visa. The check-in counter staff will verify your identity and issue baggage tags.

Rearrange the words:

[I / have / exceeded / my / baggage / allowance / at / the / airport]

I have exceeded my baggage allowance at the airport.

Describe the picture:

Passengers queue at check-in counters. Travelers may struggle with language or overweight baggage. Digital boards display flight info while staff assist confused passengers.

Questions · Your experience:

Have you ever missed a connecting flight? What happened?

In Frankfurt I missed my connection due to delays. The airline rebooked me and provided meal vouchers. I now allow 3 hours for international connections.

Compare the pictures:

Deeper questions:

How can airports improve check-in for international travelers?

Multilingual staff, clearer signage, mobile apps, and better baggage systems help. Priority lanes for elderly reduce bottlenecks. Real-time SMS notifications would improve experience.

Speaking · 2 minutes:

Describe the most efficient airport you've traveled through. What made it stand out?

Zurich airport impressed with punctuality and multilingual signage. The train to city center was convenient. Lounges and fast Wi-Fi suit business travelers well.

Vocabulary review - B2+ words:

checked in · weigh in · boarding time · gate number · final call

Grammar · Passive voice:

Your passport is checked at border control.
Your luggage is weighed at the counter.
The flight was delayed due to weather.

I was issued a new boarding pass. Security checks were enhanced. The delay was announced over the speakers.

Useful expressions:

"May I have an upgrade?" · "Fee for excess baggage?" · "Where is gate XYZ?" · "Can I check in online?"

Cultural tip:

In Italy, greet airport staff with "Buongiorno". Politeness helps enormously. Italians value personal connection before business matters.
