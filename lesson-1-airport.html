Speaking · answer in full sentences

Tell me about your last flight — what was the most stressful moment, and how did you get through it?

Aim for 60–90 seconds. Use past tenses for the events and present perfect for the result.

Model answerOn my last flight the departure board showed a two-hour delay, which cut my connection in Frankfurt to twenty minutes. I spoke to the gate agent, who rebooked me on a later flight and printed a new boarding pass. I now check the airline app before leaving home and I leave a longer gap between connections.

Rearrange the words

[ I / have / exceeded / my / baggage / allowance / at / the / airport ]

Say the sentence out loud before you check it.

AnswerI have exceeded my baggage allowance at the airport.

Describe the picture

Model descriptionPassengers are queuing at a row of check-in counters. Some are pulling suitcases, and a few are checking their phones or their documents while they wait. Staff behind the counters are helping them. The hall is bright and modern, with signs hanging above the desks.

Speaking · your experience

Have you ever been stranded somewhere unexpected while travelling? Walk me through that day, moment by moment.

Sequence it: first → then → in the end.

Model answerA storm closed the airport in Milan, so my flight was cancelled. We waited at the gate, then queued at the airline desk, where they gave us meal vouchers and a hotel room for the night. The next morning we boarded at nine. It was frustrating, but the staff kept us informed, and that made it much easier.

Compare the two pictures

Compare them out loud: what is similar, what is different, and which would you prefer?

Model comparisonBoth photos show check-in areas, but they feel very different. The empty hall has rows of traditional counters and a lot of space, so it feels calm and quiet. The other photo shows a passenger using a self-service kiosk, with screens and machines instead of a long desk, which feels faster and more automated.

Deeper question

If you could redesign one part of your last airport experience, what would it be and why?

Use second conditional: If I could…, I would…

Model answerIf I could redesign one thing, I would change security. The lanes were confusing and the bins ran out, so people queued twice. Clearer signs, more staff during peak hours and a separate lane for families would cut the stress.

Long turn · speak for 2 minutes

Describe an airport that surprised you — good or bad. What stuck with you, and would you go back?

Structure it: where → what happened → why it stayed with you.

Model answerZurich surprised me in a good way. Everything ran on time, the signs were in several languages, and a train reached the city centre in ten minutes. The lounge was quiet, with fast Wi-Fi and good coffee. It costs more than other airports, but I would choose it again for an early flight.

Grammar · the passive

Airports are full of procedures — and procedures are usually expressed in the passive.

Your passport is checked at border control.
Your luggage is weighed at the counter.
The flight was delayed because of the weather.

More passivesI was issued a new boarding pass. Security checks were enhanced after the incident. The delay was announced over the speakers, and passengers were given meal vouchers.

Cultural tip · in Italy

Greet staff with Buongiorno (morning / afternoon) or Buonasera (evening) before you ask for anything.

Italians value a personal connection before business. A short greeting and a smile open doors — then switch to English, and speak slowly. If you are stuck, Posso fare il check-in? is understood almost everywhere.
